Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Summary of Google OAuth Data Use: Kstock only collects and stores your full name and email address when you authenticate with Google Sign-In. We do not access, store, or share any other personal Google account data, and we never sell your personal information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Kstock ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights when using our stock analytics, portfolio management, and market tracking services.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in strict data minimization. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver our service:

  • Account Information: When you register directly or sign in using Google OAuth 2.0, we collect only your Full Name and Email Address, along with your Google User ID for authentication verification.
  • Authentication Credentials: If you sign up using password authentication, passwords are cryptographically hashed and salted before storage.
  • User-Generated Application Data: Market watchlists, user research notes, custom price alert triggers, and simulated portfolio transaction records you voluntarily create within your personal workspace.

Information We Do NOT Collect: We do NOT request or access sensitive Google scopes such as your Google Drive, Gmail, Google Contacts, Google Calendar, payment details, or location data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your identity and securely manage your login sessions.
  • Display your user profile (name and email) in the account dashboard.
  • Associate and maintain your personalized watchlists, notes, and alerts.
  • Deliver critical account communications, such as password reset instructions or verification emails.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy & Limited Use

Kstock's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We only access user data (name and email) requested through standard Google OAuth 2.0 profile and email scopes.
  • We do not transfer or disclose Google user data to third parties, except as strictly necessary to operate our authentication service or comply with applicable laws.
  • We do not use Google user data to serve advertisements or retarget ads.
  • We do not use or allow humans to read user data obtained through Google APIs unless required by law, needed for system security, or with your explicit consent.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosure

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your name, email, or personal research notes with advertisers or data brokers. Data is only processed by our secure backend hosting infrastructure to provide service operations.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your personal data, including:

  • All network traffic is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Cryptographic hashing of user credentials.
  • Scoped token authentication with short-lived tokens and secure session invalidation.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your name, email, and workspace data only for as long as your account remains active. You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time. Upon your request, your profile, notes, and alerts will be permanently removed from our databases.
